# Shipping Policy

## Transit estimates
Standard shipping is estimated at 3 to 7 days. This range is an estimate, not a guarantee, and cannot establish an exact arrival date without a verified dispatch anchor. A carrier estimate from current tracking records takes precedence when available. Do not invent a missing carrier estimate.

## Tracking
Order and carrier event records establish shipment state. Report the latest event and timestamp. With no carrier event for 5 or more calendar days at the scenario date, mark tracking stale; it is not proof of loss or delivery. Escalate unresolved cases rather than inventing a live update.

## Dispatch
Only pending or processing orders are pre-dispatch cancellation candidates. Do not promise a dispatch date that is absent from verified records. International shipping is not supported by this synthetic policy.

## Guarantees
Shipping is not guaranteed. Weather/distance API estimates are deferred and are not an authoritative source for the initial product.
