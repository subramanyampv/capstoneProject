# Warranty Policy

## Durations
Footwear and apparel carry a 90-day warranty, electronics 365 days, and home products 30 days. Perishable grocery products carry no warranty (0 days). Durations are configured in policy_rules.json. Do not convert a missing specification into a coverage promise.

## Claims
Ask for the order reference and a description of the defect; verify the product category. Coverage details not defined here require human review. Injury complaints must be sent to the safety queue, without medical advice or demands for medical documentation. No warranty claim is executed by the agent.
