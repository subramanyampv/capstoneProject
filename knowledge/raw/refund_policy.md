# Refund Policy

## Review threshold
The configured automatic-review threshold is $100. Refund amounts above $100 require human review; the agent cannot bypass that threshold. O-1006's $250 knife set therefore requires review. At or below $100, eligibility still requires verified records and all applicable return rules. The evaluated agent issues no refunds at any amount.

## Methods
Refund-method and processing-time details are not defined in this synthetic corpus. Do not invent payment destinations or processing durations; refer to human support for those details.

## Delayed shipments
Use current tracking events and shipping policy to explain delays. A delay alone does not prove refund eligibility. Above-threshold amounts require review even when a parcel is delayed. Escalate unresolved delivery/refund requests with evidence references; never guarantee compensation.
