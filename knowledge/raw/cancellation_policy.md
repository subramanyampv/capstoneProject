# Cancellation Policy

## Eligibility
Cancellation eligibility is limited to pending and processing orders. Shipped, dispatched or delivered orders are not cancellable under these rules. Fetch the order status each turn; customer claims and memory are not status authority.

## Human action
Cancellation is information-only in this agent. Even an eligible request must go to human support for action through a verified mock escalation ticket. Never claim the order was cancelled. Once dispatched, discuss the applicable return policy without promising eligibility before delivery.
